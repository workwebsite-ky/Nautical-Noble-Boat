FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a full vessel detailing take?+
Depending on vessel size and condition, a full detail typically takes 4–12 hours. Our Sailor package for smaller boats is usually completed in half a day, while larger yachts under the Admiral package may require 1–2 days.
Do you come to my marina or do I bring the boat to you?+
Both options are available. Our mobile teams can travel to your marina for most services. For major overhauls or dry-dock work, we may arrange vessel transport to our facility. Travel fees may apply based on distance.
Are your cleaning products safe for the marine environment?+
Absolutely. We exclusively use EPA-approved, biodegradable, non-toxic marine cleaning products. We adhere to zero-discharge protocols and are proud members of the Clean Boating Foundation.
Can I customize a service package for my specific vessel?+
Yes! All our packages are highly customizable. Simply contact us with your vessel details and requirements, and our specialists will build a bespoke service plan and quote within 24 hours.
Do you offer subscription or retainer plans?+
We offer monthly, quarterly, and annual retainer plans with priority scheduling and discounted rates. These are ideal for yacht owners who want year-round vessel care without the hassle of repeated bookings.
What areas and ports do you currently operate in?+
We currently operate in all major Gulf marinas and have partner crews in 30+ countries. Contact us for availability in your specific port — we're continuously expanding our global network.
